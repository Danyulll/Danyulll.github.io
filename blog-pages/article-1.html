Bayesian statistics provides a powerful framework for reasoning under uncertainty. By incorporating prior knowledge and updating beliefs with new evidence, we can make more informed decisions in complex scenarios. This approach is particularly valuable in machine learning, where we often deal with limited data and need to quantify uncertainty.
